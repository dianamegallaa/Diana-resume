I am a second-year Pre-Business student at the University of California, Riverside, interested in finance, investment banking, and business strategy. I enjoy learning how businesses operate, how financial decisions influence companies, and how individuals and organizations can use financial knowledge to make informed decisions.

Through my involvement in campus organizations, I have been able to explore my professional interests while developing leadership, communication, and teamwork skills. I am involved in FBLA, where I serve as Director of Professional Development, and Hylander Financial Group. These experiences allow me to connect with other students, learn from people with different backgrounds, and become more familiar with professional opportunities in business and finance.

As Director of Professional Development for FBLA, I help support programming that prepares students for internships, professional networking, and future career opportunities. I value creating opportunities for students to build confidence and learn practical skills that they can use beyond college. I believe professional development is important because success involves more than academic knowledge. It also requires communication, preparation, and the willingness to keep learning.

My work experiences have also helped me understand the importance of responsibility, adaptability, and working with different people. Through customer-facing work and sales experience, I have developed my ability to communicate clearly, respond to challenges, and work toward goals. I have learned that building trust and understanding people's needs are important parts of successful business relationships.

Outside of academics and professional development, I enjoy playing soccer, participating in flag football, going to the gym, cooking, playing pickleball, and hiking. Staying active has taught me discipline, consistency, and persistence. Whether I am working toward a personal fitness goal or learning something new, I try to approach challenges with a willingness to improve. I carry these same values into my academic and professional goals.

Looking ahead, I hope to continue building my knowledge of financial markets, company valuation, and business strategy while gaining experience through internships and mentorship. I am particularly interested in learning more about investment banking and financial advising. My goal is to build a strong foundation in finance, learn from experienced professionals, and develop the analytical and interpersonal skills necessary to contribute to a team.

I see college as an opportunity to challenge myself, explore different career paths, and build meaningful connections. I am excited to continue learning through coursework, extracurricular involvement, and hands-on professional experiences as I work toward a career in business and finance.
